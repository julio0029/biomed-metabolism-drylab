Teaching notes and selected references

Stoichiometric teaching model. The arithmetic displayed here uses 2 pyruvate per glucose, 46 pumped protons per oxidised pyruvate, 1 O₂ per 10 pumped protons, and approximately 3 protons per ATP. The fatty-acid slider contributes a relative oxidation signal because fatty-acid chain length is not specified. These are deliberately simplified teaching constants, not a universal account of mitochondrial bioenergetics. ROS reduces the modelled coupling efficiency and therefore the displayed P:O ratio and ATP output.

The DKA teaching pattern adapts the severe or uncontrolled diabetes profile used in BIOSCI358 Lab 3. It is a deliberately simplified prompt, not a diagnosis or a representation of an individual patient. The lab profile distinguishes DKA from uncontrolled type 2 diabetes or hyperosmolar hyperglycaemic state; this page does not model insulin signalling, renal glucose handling, ketogenesis or acid base balance.

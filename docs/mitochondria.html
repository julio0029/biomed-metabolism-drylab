The mitochondrial membrane.

Complex I and Complex II feed electrons into the shared Q-pool. Electrons then pass through Complex III, cytochrome c and Complex IV, where oxygen is reduced to water. Complexes I and IV translocate protons from matrix to IMS, while Complex III releases QH₂ derived protons into the IMS.

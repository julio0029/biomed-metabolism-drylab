The mitochondrial membrane.

Complex I and Complex II feed electrons into the shared Q-pool. Electrons then pass through Complex III, cytochrome c and Complex IV, where oxygen is reduced to water. This first diagram is a deliberately flat pathway map. Real cristae are folded, and respiratory complexes assemble into supercomplexes.

The TCA wheel.

Inside mitochondria, small fuel pieces join a circular pathway. Carbon enters, carbon dioxide leaves, and energy is captured in carrier molecules such as NADH.

Follow one turn of the wheel

This is a simplified carbon story. Each blue bead is one carbon. Amber beads are carbon dioxide leaving the wheel. Cyan capsules are NADH, an energy carrying molecule.

1. Fuel enters

Pyruvate from carbohydrate, or small fuel pieces from lipid and ketone routes, can feed the wheel.

2. Carbon leaves

Two carbons leave as carbon dioxide during a turn. The wheel is ready to receive more fuel.

3. Energy is carried

NADH stores captured energy. It carries this to the inner mitochondrial membrane.

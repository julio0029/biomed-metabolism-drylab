With food fuel and oxygen available, mitochondria can make ATP, reusable packets that help cells do their jobs.

Words to take forward

Food fuel

Food is broken into small pieces that cells can use. Glucose is one common fuel.

Oxygen

Oxygen helps the mitochondrial power station get the most energy from fuel.

ATP

ATP is a small, reusable energy packet. Cells spend it to move, build and communicate.
